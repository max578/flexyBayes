flexyBayes — validation, walkthrough & primer

flexyBayes specifies Bayesian mixed models with ASReml- or brms-style formula syntax and estimates them through the greta, INLA, or brms backends. This bundle validates that the one-formula, three-engine design is faithful, and teaches the ideas behind it.

The one result to remember. Through the INLA backend, flexyBayes is numerically identical to a hand-written INLA::inla() fit, and across all three Bayesian engines plus the frequentist REML reference the headline sleepstudy slope lands at ≈ 10.5 ms per lost day of sleep — agreement across paradigms.
