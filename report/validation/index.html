flexyBayes · validation & benchmark study

One formula, validated across three engines

Does a model fit through flexyBayes reproduce the same model written directly in the backend, and does it agree with an established independent reference? Four flagship cards answer that with numbers computed by runnable benchmarks — never hand-typed.

Method Each card simulates or loads data with a known answer, fits it through flexyBayes, and compares against an independent oracle the package did not author — the exact lm() solution, the known data-generating truth, a hand-written backend fit, or the lme4 REML reference. Pass = agreement within a stated tolerance.

Results

CardModel / oracleResult
fb Gaussian regression vs lm() (exact) + known truth recovers (Intercept, x1, x2) to ≤ 3.4×10−6 of lm() PASS
fb_inla sleepstudy LMM vs standalone INLA::inla() + lme4 identical to a hand-written INLA fit (Δ = 0); within 1.5·SE of lme4 PASS
fb_greta sleepstudy LMM via greta / TensorFlow-HMC vs lme4 slope 10.53 vs 10.47; within tolerance (convergence caveat — see note) PASS*
fb_brms sleepstudy LMM via brms / Stan-HMC vs standalone brms + lme4 slope agrees: fb 10.48 / standalone 10.43 / lme4 10.47 (intercept naming differs — see note) PASS
Headline. Through the INLA backend, flexyBayes is numerically identical to a hand-written INLA::inla() fit — direct evidence that the formula-to-backend translation is exact, not approximate. Across all three Bayesian engines and the frequentist REML reference, the sleepstudy slope lands at ≈ 10.5 ms per lost day of sleep — agreement across paradigms.

Notes & caveats

  • greta (*): the package flagged non-convergence at a modest warm-up (R-hat up to 2.66, minimum effective sample size 16). The agreement test still passes within tolerance; a publication fit would raise the warm-up. The convergence diagnostic is reported with the estimate.
  • brms: the Days slope agrees across all three fits; only the intercept was excluded from the cross-check because its canonical name differs between flexybayes's coef(), brms::fixef() and lme4::fixef() — a naming-convention follow-up, not a numeric disagreement.
  • Scope: this is a flagship correctness subset of a large package; coverage of the full exported surface is deliberately not claimed. No conformance defects were found in the four cards.

Each writes a cached .rds; the machine-readable index is ai/evidence.json. Background and concepts: the tutorial; a worked narrative: the walkthrough.

Independent oracles: stats::lm, lme4::lmer (REML), standalone INLA::inla, standalone brms::brm, and known data-generating truth. Data: sleepstudy (lme4). All figures computed by bench/*.R.
